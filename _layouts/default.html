Frank Chang

I am a 3rd year undergraduate student in the College of Computing at Georgia Tech. I currently work on robotic foundation models in the PAIR Lab led by Animesh Garg.

I previously spent two summers at AWS working on their data pipeline, and have also worked at MITRE's Emerging Technology Innovation Center on naturalistic adversarial patches.

Outside of school I enjoy backpacking and mountaineering. My goal is to visit every national park in the US, and I have currently visited 21. I am aiming for a 6000m summit in 2027!

Research

I'm interested in computer vision, deep learning, generative AI, and image processing. Most of my research is about inferring the physical world (shape, motion, color, light, etc) from images, usually with radiance fields.
